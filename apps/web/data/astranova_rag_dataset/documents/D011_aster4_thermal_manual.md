# Aster-4 Thermal Control Manual

- Document ID: D011_ASTER4_THERMAL_MANUAL
- Document type: operations manual
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

The Aster-4 optical payload maximum operating temperature is 36 °C according to the current approved spacecraft specification, Revision 2.

For routine imaging, the operations target is 30 °C or below. At 34 °C, suspend new imaging requests and begin thermal recovery. At 36 °C, imaging is prohibited until the payload returns below 32 °C and Mission Operations authorises resumption.

The 38 °C value appearing in Revision 1 is superseded and must not be used.
