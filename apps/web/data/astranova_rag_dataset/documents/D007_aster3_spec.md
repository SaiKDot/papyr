# Aster-3 Spacecraft Specification

- Document ID: D007_ASTER3_SPEC
- Document type: technical specification
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

Aster-3 spacecraft baseline:
- Nominal mass: 94 kg
- Battery usable capacity at beginning of life: 1.55 kWh
- Average payload power in imaging mode: 125 W
- Maximum operating temperature for the optical payload: 35 °C
- Communications: S-band and X-band downlink
- Design life: 4 years

Aster-3 uses a fixed battery assembly. Do not apply Aster-4 battery maintenance procedures to Aster-3.
