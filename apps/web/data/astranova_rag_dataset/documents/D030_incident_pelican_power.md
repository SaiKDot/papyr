# Incident IR-029 — Pelican Demonstration Power Conflict

- Document ID: D030_INCIDENT_PELICAN_POWER
- Document type: incident report
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

On 6 October 2026, an integrated rehearsal showed that simultaneous imaging and relay operation would breach the conservative energy allocation. No hardware fault occurred. The root cause was an incompatible activity schedule. Corrective action: split the demonstration into imaging followed by relay-only operation and preserve the 15% reserve. Customer accepted the revised sequence on 7 October. Status: closed.
