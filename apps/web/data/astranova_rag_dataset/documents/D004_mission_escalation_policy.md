# Mission Escalation Policy

- Document ID: D004_MISSION_ESCALATION_POLICY
- Document type: policy
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

A spacecraft anomaly that threatens a mission milestone by more than 24 hours must be escalated to the Mission Operations Director and the relevant Project Lead within four working hours of confirmation.

A safety-critical anomaly requires immediate suspension of the affected procedure. The Quality and Reliability Lead must open an incident record within one working day.

A schedule change exceeding five calendar days requires Customer Programmes approval. A budget increase greater than 5% requires Finance approval.
