# Document Control and Information Policy

- Document ID: D003_INFORMATION_CONTROL_POLICY
- Document type: policy
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

Controlled technical specifications must carry a revision number and an approval status. When two controlled documents disagree, use the latest approved revision, not the newest draft or the document with the latest file date.

An engineering change is not effective until it has an approved Engineering Change Notice (ECN). Meeting minutes record decisions but do not themselves replace a controlled specification.

All mission dates are stated in UTC unless a document explicitly says otherwise. Costs are in US dollars (USD).
