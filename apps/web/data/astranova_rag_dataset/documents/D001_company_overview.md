# AstraNova Systems — Company Overview

AstraNova Systems designs and operates compact Earth-observation satellites for agricultural and coastal monitoring. Its headquarters is in Port Meridian, a fictional city. The company has three operating groups: Spacecraft Engineering, Mission Operations, and Customer Programmes.

The current spacecraft family is the Aster series: Aster-2, Aster-3 and Aster-4. Aster-4 is the newest model. The internal project names Orion, Kestrel and Pelican refer to customer missions, not spacecraft models.

The Chief Executive Officer is Mira Voss. The Head of Spacecraft Engineering is Dev Malik. The Mission Operations Director is Lena Ortiz. The Finance Controller is Arun Bell.
