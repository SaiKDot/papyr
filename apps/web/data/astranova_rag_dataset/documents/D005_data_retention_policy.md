# Engineering Data Retention Policy

- Document ID: D005_DATA_RETENTION_POLICY
- Document type: policy
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

Raw telemetry is retained for a minimum of 24 months. Processed mission summaries are retained for seven years. Signed acceptance certificates and approved Engineering Change Notices are retained for the operational life of the spacecraft plus ten years.

Working drafts may be deleted after the approved revision is published, provided the approval history remains accessible.
