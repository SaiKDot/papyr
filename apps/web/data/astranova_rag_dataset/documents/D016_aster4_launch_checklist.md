# Aster-4 Pre-Launch Checklist

- Document ID: D016_ASTER4_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST
- Document type: procedure
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

The pre-launch review requires:
1. Approved spacecraft configuration and current specification revision.
2. Battery health report showing at least 95% of the mission-specific expected capacity.
3. Communications test on S-band and X-band.
4. Optical payload thermal test.
5. Open anomaly review signed by Quality and Mission Operations.
6. Customer acceptance of the final mission timeline.

No spacecraft may be declared launch-ready while a safety-critical anomaly remains open.
