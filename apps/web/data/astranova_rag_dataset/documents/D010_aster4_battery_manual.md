# Aster-4 Battery Maintenance Manual

- Document ID: D010_ASTER4_BATTERY_MANUAL
- Document type: operations manual
- Organisation: AstraNova Systems (fictional)
- Classification: Internal training dataset
- Status: Approved for RAG testing

The Aster-4 battery has a usable beginning-of-life capacity of 1.80 kWh. For mission planning, use 85% of this value as the planning capacity unless the current telemetry health report specifies a lower value.

The standard planning capacity is therefore 1.53 kWh. Do not assume that the battery can supply the full nameplate capacity during an eclipse; retain a 15% reserve.

Battery temperatures must remain between 5 °C and 30 °C during charging. If a cell temperature exceeds 30 °C, stop charging and notify Mission Operations.
