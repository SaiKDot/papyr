Based on , self-attention (or intra-attention) relates distinct positions of a single sequence to compute a contextual representation:

Query, Key, Value Triplet: Every input token vector is projected into three distinct spaces via linear transformations: $Q$, $K$, and $V$.

Scaled Dot-Product: Calculates the dot product of $Q$ with $K^T$, scaled by $\sqrt{d_k}$ to prevent gradient vanishing in large dimensions.

Softmax Weighting: A softmax distribution is applied over the keys to output a weighted sum across all value vectors ($V$).
